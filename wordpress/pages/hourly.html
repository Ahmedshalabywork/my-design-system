Hourly Limo and Sprinter Charter in Charleston

Mecca Limo offers hourly limo and black car charters in Charleston, SC, 24/7. Book a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter for up to 14 passengers, a luxury SUV for up to 6, or an executive sedan, and your professional chauffeur stays with you for every stop of the day or night.

How hourly charters work

Instead of booking separate rides, you reserve a vehicle and chauffeur for a block of time. Your chauffeur waits while you shop, dine, tour or celebrate, and takes you to the next stop whenever you are ready. Hourly minimums apply; for example, Sprinter charters have a 3-hour minimum.
