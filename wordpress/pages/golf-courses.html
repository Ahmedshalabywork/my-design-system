Golf trip transportation services

Airport to resort with your clubs

We pick up at Charleston International Airport (CHS) or Charleston Executive Airport (JZI) and take you straight to Kiawah, Wild Dunes or downtown, with room for every golf bag.

Round-trip to the tee

Drop-off before your tee time and pickup when you finish, or book by the hour and have your chauffeur wait.

Golf weekends and corporate outings

Multi-day buddy trips, bachelor golf weekends and corporate golf outings: we schedule every ride, from rounds to dinners.

Vehicles with room for clubs

  • Luxury SUV: the Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban, GMC Yukon Denali or Lincoln Navigator fits a foursome with clubs.
  • Mercedes-Benz Sprinter: up to 14 passengers with room for golf bags, for larger groups.
  • Executive sedan: up to 3 passengers for a quick ride without the clubs.

Tell us how many golfers and bags you have when you book and we will match the vehicle. See the full fleet.

Golf transportation pricing

Point-to-point and airport rides are quoted as a flat price. If you want your chauffeur to wait, hourly rates start at $100 per hour for a luxury SUV and $135 for the Sprinter, with a 3-hour minimum. See our booking policy.
