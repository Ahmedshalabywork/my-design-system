Charleston Airport Car Service (CHS) 24/7

Mecca Limo runs private airport transfers to and from Charleston International Airport (CHS), Charleston Executive Airport (JZI) and Mount Pleasant Regional Airport (LRO), 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Your chauffeur tracks your flight, waits for you on arrival, loads your bags and takes you straight to your hotel, home, venue or meeting. No rideshare queue, no surge pricing and no guessing which car will show up.

Book your ride by calling or texting (843) 804-1188, or request a free quote with your flight details.

How your airport pickup works

  1. Book ahead. Send us your flight number, arrival time, passenger count and luggage. We confirm the vehicle and the price before your trip.
  2. We track your flight. Your chauffeur follows your flight, so an early arrival or a delay is already accounted for.
  3. 20 minutes of wait time included. Every airport pickup includes 20 minutes of wait time from landing in the price, enough to reach baggage claim without rushing.
  4. Your chauffeur handles the bags. We load your luggage, golf clubs or car seats and get you on the road.
  5. Straight to your door. Downtown hotel, beach house, island resort or office, wherever you are headed.

Airports we serve

Charleston International Airport (CHS)

The main commercial airport for Charleston, North Charleston and the Lowcountry. We handle arrivals and departures around the clock, including the first flights of the morning and late-night arrivals.

Charleston Executive Airport (JZI)

The private aviation airport on Johns Island, close to Kiawah Island, Seabrook Island and Wadmalaw. Flying in privately? We meet you at the FBO, such as Atlantic Aviation, and drive you to Kiawah, Wild Dunes or downtown.

Mount Pleasant Regional Airport (LRO)

A convenient general aviation airport for Mount Pleasant, Isle of Palms and Wild Dunes.

We also drive to and from other airports outside the Charleston area for one-way and round-trip transfers. Ask for a quote.

Choose the right vehicle for your bags

Airport trips are about luggage as much as passengers. Every vehicle in our fleet is late-model and detailed before each booking.

  • Executive sedan: up to 3 passengers with 2 bags. Ideal for business travelers and couples.
  • Luxury SUV: up to 6 passengers with luggage. The Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban, GMC Yukon Denali and Lincoln Navigator are favorites for families and golf groups with clubs.
  • Mercedes-Benz Sprinter: up to 14 passengers with room for luggage. Perfect for wedding guests, corporate teams and group trips.

Corporate and executive airport transfers

Visiting executives, clients and conference speakers need a ride that is on time and quiet enough to work in. We coordinate pickups for every arrival on a trip, keep one point of contact for your team, and can hold a vehicle between meetings. Learn more about our corporate car service.

Wedding guests and group arrivals

Out-of-town guests arriving for a Charleston wedding? We pick them up at CHS, take them to their hotels and bring them back to the airport after the weekend. Pair it with wedding day transportation so everyone is covered.

Airport car service vs. rideshare at CHS

  • Guaranteed ride: your vehicle is reserved, even for a 5 a.m. departure or a midnight arrival.
  • Price agreed upfront: no surge pricing after a big event or a busy holiday weekend.
  • The right vehicle: room for every bag and every passenger in one car.
  • A professional chauffeur: flight tracking, help with bags and local knowledge.
