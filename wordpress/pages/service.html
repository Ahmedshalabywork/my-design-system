What does a black car service include?

Black car service is private, pre-booked transportation in a luxury vehicle driven by a professional chauffeur. Unlike a taxi or a rideshare app, the ride is reserved in advance for you alone, the vehicle and driver are matched to your trip, and the price is agreed before you get in. With Mecca Limo, every booking includes:

  • A reserved vehicle and chauffeur. Your car is scheduled for your pickup time, not dispatched from whoever happens to be nearby.
  • A professional chauffeur. Our chauffeurs know Charleston, the islands and the airport, and they handle bags, doors and directions so you do not have to.
  • Privacy and a quiet cabin. Make calls, prepare for a meeting or simply rest between stops.
  • Room for luggage and gear. We match the vehicle to your bags, golf clubs, strollers or wedding attire, not just your headcount.
  • Multiple stops on one booking. Hotel, restaurant, venue and back again, all on one reservation.

Black car service vs. rideshare in Charleston

Rideshare apps work for short, casual trips. A black car is the better choice when timing, comfort or the impression you make matters:

  • Reliability: your car is reserved ahead of time, so there is no surge pricing or waiting for a driver to accept at 4 a.m. or after a big event.
  • Vehicle and space: you choose a sedan, SUV or Sprinter that fits your group and luggage, instead of hoping the right car shows up.
  • The chauffeur: a professional who knows the area, helps with bags and waits for you between stops.
  • Groups: one Sprinter keeps up to 14 people together instead of splitting into several cars.
