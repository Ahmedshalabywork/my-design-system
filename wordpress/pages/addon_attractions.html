How a private Charleston tour works

Book a vehicle and chauffeur by the hour and build your own route. Tell us what you want to see, whether that is the historic district, the gardens and plantations along the Ashley River, the beaches or a mix, and your chauffeur drives you from stop to stop and waits while you explore.

  • Your pace: stay as long as you like at each stop.
  • No parking: we drop you at the door, even downtown.
  • Local knowledge: our chauffeurs know the city and the best order to see it.
