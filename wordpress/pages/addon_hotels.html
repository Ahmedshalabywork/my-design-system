How your hotel transfer works

  1. Book with your flight number and hotel name.
  2. We track your flight and your chauffeur meets you at Charleston International Airport (CHS), with 20 minutes of wait time from landing included.
  3. Straight to your hotel: a private ride, not a shared shuttle with stops at other hotels.
  4. Return trip: we pick you up at the hotel for your flight home or your cruise.

Hotel transfer vehicles and pricing

An executive sedan fits up to 3 passengers with 2 bags, a luxury SUV up to 6 with luggage, and the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter up to 14. Hotel transfers are quoted as a flat price, and you know it before you book. See our booking policy and fleet.
