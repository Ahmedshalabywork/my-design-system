How your CHS pickup works

  1. Book ahead and share your flight number. We use it to track your flight, so your pickup time moves with an early or late landing.
  2. Land and turn your phone on. Your chauffeur contacts you when your flight arrives and tells you exactly where to meet.
  3. Collect your bags. Airport pickups include 20 minutes of wait time from landing, enough for most baggage claims.
  4. Ride straight to your door. Your chauffeur loads your luggage and drives you to your hotel, home, rental or venue.

Drive times from CHS

Typical times by car from Charleston International Airport. Traffic, bridges and time of day can add more.

  • Downtown Charleston: about 20 to 25 minutes
  • Daniel Island: about 15 to 20 minutes
  • Summerville: about 25 to 35 minutes
  • Mount Pleasant: about 25 to 30 minutes
  • Isle of Palms and Sullivan's Island: about 30 to 40 minutes
  • Folly Beach: about 30 to 40 minutes
  • Kiawah Island and Seabrook Island: about 50 to 60 minutes

Which vehicle should you book?

  • Executive sedan: up to 3 passengers with 2 bags. Best for business travelers and couples.
  • Luxury SUV: up to 6 passengers with luggage. Best for families and golf bags.
  • Mercedes-Benz Sprinter: up to 14 passengers with luggage. Best for wedding parties, teams and bachelorette groups.

Tips for a smooth arrival

  • Book as soon as you have your flight, especially for spring and fall weekends and holidays.
  • Tell us about extra bags, golf clubs, strollers or child seats when you book so we send the right vehicle.
  • Traveling with a group on different flights? Send every flight number and we will plan the pickups.
  • Save our number, (843) 804-1188, so you can text us if anything changes.
