Charleston brewery areas to explore

The upper peninsula

Several breweries and taprooms sit close together north of downtown, which makes this area an easy place to start a crawl.

North Charleston and Park Circle

A growing neighborhood of taprooms, beer gardens and food trucks.

James Island and Johns Island

Laid-back breweries on the way to Folly Beach and Kiawah, good for pairing a beach morning with an afternoon tasting.

Mount Pleasant

Breweries across the Cooper River, a short drive from Shem Creek and Isle of Palms.

Taproom hours change by season and many close one or two days a week, so check each brewery's hours before you finalize your route. We are happy to help plan the order of stops to cut down on drive time.

How many breweries and how many hours?

Three to four breweries in about four hours is a comfortable pace for most groups, with time to taste, eat and enjoy each stop. Hourly bookings have a 3-hour minimum, and you can add time on the day.
