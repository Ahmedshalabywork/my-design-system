How to plan your wedding transportation

  1. Count your riders. List who needs a ride: the couple, the wedding party, family and any guests you want to shuttle.
  2. Map the day. Note every stop: hotel, getting-ready location, ceremony, photos, reception and the final drop-off.
  3. Add buffer time. Charleston traffic, bridges and beach roads can be slow on weekends. We build in time so you are never late.
  4. Choose your vehicles. We will suggest the right mix based on your headcount and schedule.
  5. Book early. Spring and fall weekends fill months ahead, so reserve as soon as you have your date.
