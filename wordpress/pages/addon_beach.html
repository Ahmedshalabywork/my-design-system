How beach transportation works

We drop your group at the beach and pick you up when you are ready, or book by the hour and keep your chauffeur nearby. No hunting for parking on a summer weekend, and nobody has to drive home after a long day in the sun.

  • Beaches we serve: Folly Beach, Sullivan's Island, Isle of Palms, Kiawah Island, Seabrook Island and more.
  • Room for gear: chairs, coolers and beach bags fit in our SUVs and Sprinter.
  • Airport to the beach: we pick up at CHS and drive straight to your beach rental.
