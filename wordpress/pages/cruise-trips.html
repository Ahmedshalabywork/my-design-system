Charleston Cruise Port Transportation

Starting or ending a cruise in Charleston? Mecca Limo provides private transfers between the airport, your hotel and the Charleston cruise terminal downtown, with room for everyone's luggage. We are family-owned and available 24/7, so early embarkation mornings and late flights home are never a problem.

Call or text (843) 804-1188 or request a free quote.

Cruise transfers we provide

  • Airport to cruise terminal: from Charleston International Airport (CHS) straight to the ship.
  • Hotel to cruise terminal: from downtown, Mount Pleasant or North Charleston hotels.
  • Cruise terminal to the airport: pickup when you disembark, timed to your flight.
  • Home pickups: from Mount Pleasant, North Charleston, Summerville, Goose Creek and nearby towns.

How a cruise day transfer works

  1. Book with your details: ship, sailing date, pickup address or flight number, passenger count and bags.
  2. We time the ride: arriving for your boarding window, not too early or late.
  3. Help with luggage: your chauffeur loads and unloads every bag.
  4. Return day: we meet you when the ship comes back and take you to the airport, your hotel or home.

See Charleston before you sail

Arriving a day early? Add a sightseeing tour of the historic district, gardens and plantations, or book by the hour and explore at your own pace.

Room for the whole group

  • Mercedes-Benz Sprinter: up to 14 passengers with room for luggage, ideal for family reunions and group cruises.
  • Luxury SUV: up to 6 passengers with luggage, such as the Lincoln Navigator and GMC Yukon Denali.
  • Executive sedan: up to 3 passengers with 2 bags.

Cruise transfer pricing

Cruise transfers are quoted as a flat price based on your pickup point, vehicle and time, and you know the price before you book. For a sightseeing stop or extra time, hourly rates start at $85 per hour for a sedan, $100 for an SUV and $135 for the Sprinter, with a 3-hour minimum. See our booking policy.
