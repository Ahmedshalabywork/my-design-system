Prom and homecoming limo service

Prom is one of the biggest nights of the school year, and getting there together is part of the fun. Our Mercedes-Benz Sprinter seats up to 14, so a whole group of friends can ride to photos, dinner and the dance in one vehicle.

Safety rules for prom and student events

Your teens are in good hands with our experienced chauffeurs. For every student booking:

  • No drugs, alcohol or smoking in the vehicle, ever.
  • No surprise passengers. Only the riders on the booking, unless a parent approves a change in advance.
  • Set pickup and drop-off times agreed with the parent who books.
  • A professional chauffeur who stays with the vehicle and knows the venues.
