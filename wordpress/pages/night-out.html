How a party bus night works

  1. Plan your stops. Send us your pickup point, the places you want to go and roughly how long at each.
  2. Pick your hours. Most nights out are booked by the hour, so your chauffeur waits between stops.
  3. Ride together. Everyone is picked up at once and dropped off safely at the end of the night.
  4. Change of plans? Add a stop on the night; just tell your chauffeur.

Party bus vs. rideshare for groups

  • One vehicle, whole group: no splitting into four cars or losing friends between stops.
  • A chauffeur who waits: no hunting for rides at closing time.
  • A safe ride home: everyone is dropped off at the end of the night.
  • Clear pricing: no surge pricing on Saturday nights.
