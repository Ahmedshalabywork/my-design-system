Our Charleston Limo and Black Car Fleet

Mecca Limo is a family-owned limo and black car service in Charleston, SC, available 24/7. Our fleet covers every kind of trip: a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter for groups of up to 14, luxury SUVs for up to 6, and executive sedans for airport runs and business travel. Every vehicle is late-model, professionally maintained and detailed before every ride.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter: up to 14 passengers

The Sprinter is our most requested vehicle for wedding parties, bachelor and bachelorette weekends, corporate groups and nights out. It seats up to 14 passengers with room for luggage, so the whole group rides together instead of splitting into several cars.

  • Seats up to 14 with luggage space
  • Ideal for weddings, party bus nights, corporate shuttles and group airport transfers
  • Available by the hour or for one-way and round-trip rides

Luxury SUVs: up to 6 passengers

Our luxury SUVs, such as the Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban and GMC Yukon Denali, carry up to 6 passengers with luggage. They are a favorite for families, small groups, golf trips and airport transfers with several bags.

Executive sedans: up to 3 passengers

Our executive sedans seat up to 3 passengers with 2 bags. They are quiet and private, the right choice for business travel, date nights and solo airport runs to and from Charleston International Airport (CHS).

Which vehicle fits your group?

  • 1 to 3 passengers: executive sedan
  • 4 to 6 passengers, or lots of luggage: luxury SUV
  • 7 to 14 passengers: Mercedes-Benz Sprinter
  • Larger groups: combine vehicles so everyone arrives together
