Kiawah Island Car Service and Limo

Mecca Limo provides private car service and limo rides to and from Kiawah Island, SC, 24 hours a day. We drive guests between Charleston International Airport (CHS), downtown Charleston and Kiawah's resorts, villas, golf courses and wedding venues in executive sedans, luxury SUVs and a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter for up to 14 passengers.

Charleston Airport (CHS) to Kiawah Island

Kiawah Island is roughly an hour's drive from Charleston International Airport, depending on traffic. Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur will track your arrival, meet you at the airport and take you straight to your resort or rental. Airport pickups include 20 minutes of wait time from landing.

Kiawah Island weddings

We move couples, wedding parties and guests between hotels, ceremony sites, receptions and after-parties across Kiawah and the Charleston area. One family wrote that their ride from Charleston to Kiawah for their son's wedding "was made even more memorable" because the chauffeur turned the drive into a guided tour of the area.

Golf trips

Heading to the Ocean Course or Kiawah's other championship courses? We pick up your group, your bags and your clubs from the airport, your hotel or your rental and bring you back after your round. Tell us how many players and bags you have so we send the right vehicle.

Corporate groups and events on Kiawah

We handle transportation for corporate retreats and executive events on the island. One planner organizing an event for a group of CEOs in Kiawah told us Mecca Limo was "outstanding and far more reasonable" than the alternatives.
