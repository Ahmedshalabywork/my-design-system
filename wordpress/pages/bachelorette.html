A sample bachelorette weekend itinerary

Friday: arrival and welcome dinner

We pick up the group at Charleston International Airport (CHS) and drop everyone at your rental or hotel. That night, ride to dinner downtown and on to a rooftop bar or King Street.

Saturday: beach day and the big night out

Spend the day at Folly Beach, Sullivan's Island or Isle of Palms, or on the water at Shem Creek in Mount Pleasant. In the evening, book the Sprinter for dinner, drinks and dancing, with stops wherever the night goes.

Sunday: brunch and departures

One last brunch downtown, then airport drop-offs for everyone's flights home.

How many hours should you book?

Most bachelorette nights run four to six hours from the first pickup to the last drop-off. All hourly bookings have a 3-hour minimum, and you can add time on the night if plans change. For daytime outings, a shorter block or simple point-to-point rides often work better.
