Backlinks

Spam links to disavow

Hundreds of links come from link-selling networks, with anchors like "premium authority link building", "PBN network… buy backlinks cheap" and a Telegram link-shop channel, plus casino and gambling pages. Google ignores most spam on its own, but this volume is worth disavowing.

Links to earn (competitors have them, you don't)

  • Wedding sites: The Knot, WeddingWire, Brides.com, Southern Weddings, Wedding Chicks, Loverly, Equally Wed, Wed Society.
  • Local directories: Charleston.com, ChamberofCommerce.com, Yellow Pages, BBB, Nextdoor, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.
  • Partners: Charleston venues, hotels, caterers and planners you already work with, on their preferred-vendor pages.
  • Community: local charity events, for example sponsoring rides for a Charleston Animal Society event.

Never buy links. That is how the spam above happens.
