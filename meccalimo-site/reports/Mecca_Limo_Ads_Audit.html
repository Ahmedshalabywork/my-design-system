What moved since this morning's audit

Of the 28 original problems, 18 are fixed, 6 are partly fixed and 4 are open. This morning it was 11, 3 and 14. All 6 problems found after the rebuild are handled except the budget timing, which needs a few days of data.

Fixed tonight, 9:30pm to 10:05pm:

  • "rent a" is gone as a negative, so "rent a party bus" and "rent a limo" can show your ads again. "rent a car" is blocked instead.
  • Black Car & Corporate is no longer a catch-all. It now has its own negatives (bus, ferry, carriage, waymo, valet, greyhound, airport, kiawah, seabrook), so those searches go to the right group or nowhere. The two vague "transportation" keywords are paused.
  • Ad claims are accurate. "Stretch Limo Available" is gone from both wedding ads and its keyword is paused. Every ad now says "150+" reviews, matching your 153 on Google.
  • Asset clean-up is done. The keyword-stuffed "Destinations" snippet, the email callout, the duplicate snippet and the duplicate call asset are removed. Two near-duplicate quote callouts were swapped for "Room for Golf Bags" and "Cars Detailed Every Ride".
  • Tablets are back on at a normal bid. They had been turned off using the junk numbers.
  • The leftover conversion actions can't steer bidding. Google won't let the API relabel those six system actions, but this campaign's goals already exclude them.

Fixed earlier today:

  • The junk "Call" conversion is demoted. It no longer counts and no longer steers the bidding.
  • Auto-apply is off. All 23 recommendation types are paused, so Google can't turn Display back on or add keywords without asking.
  • Sitelinks are rebuilt. 20 sitelinks, each to its own page. The 11 that went to the homepage are gone.
  • Brand searches have their own ad group, so "mecca limo" searches no longer mix with cold traffic.
  • Call assets report to the right place. Calls from the ad's phone button now go to "Calls from ads".
  • Quote forms are recording. Three came through today.

What it takes to reach 100

Everything that can be fixed from here is fixed. The remaining 24 points come from three sources: two quick switches only you can flip, real-world proof that only time gives, and booking data from your dispatch system.

  1. You, 2 minutes: turn on enhanced conversions for leads. Go to Goals, then Settings, then tick "Enhanced conversions for leads" and choose "Google tag". The website already sends the hashed email and phone with each quote, so it starts working the moment you tick it. See T5. About +3 points.
  2. You, when you can: take 4 to 6 real photos of your own vehicles (Sprinter, Suburban, Escalade, sedan) outside in daylight. They become image ads and Business Profile posts. The website's current photos are stock, with logos on top and stretch limos you don't offer, so they can't be used. About +1 point.
  3. Time, about 2 weeks: let the new call tracking prove itself. Once "Website calls 60s+" records real calls, it becomes the main phone goal in place of the tap. See T2. About +4 points.
  4. Time, 3 to 4 weeks: let bidding learn and quality scores settle. Change nothing in bidding until then. See B1 and K4. About +6 points.
  5. Limo Anywhere: send booked rides and their value back to Google. This is what lets Google tell a short airport run from a full wedding day. It's the biggest single gain left. See T6. About +6 points.
  6. November 5: remove the paused clutter and decide on out-of-area targeting with 30 days of clean data. See S2 and W6. About +4 points.

Realistic path: 79 once you flip enhanced conversions, mid-80s by early November, 90 and above once booked rides flow back. A true 100 means every item proven with results, and no account gets there in a day.
