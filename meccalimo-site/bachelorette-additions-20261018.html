Bachelorette party transportation in Charleston, SC

Charleston is one of the most popular bachelorette destinations in the country, and getting a group of 8 to 14 people between a rental house, brunch, a boat, King Street and the bars is the hardest part of the weekend. Mecca Limo gives your bride tribe one private, chauffeured ride for the whole day or night: a black Mercedes-Benz Sprinter party bus for up to 14 guests, or a Chevrolet Suburban for groups of up to 6. Your chauffeur waits at every stop, so nobody gets left behind and nobody has to drive.

Which vehicle fits your bachelorette group?

  • Up to 6 guests: a black Chevrolet Suburban SUV. Great for dinner, a winery afternoon or airport pickups.
  • 7 to 14 guests: the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter party bus, with leather seating, ambient lighting and room for everyone to ride together.
  • 15+ guests: two vehicles that travel together on the same schedule.

Favorite bachelorette stops we drive to

  • Shem Creek in Mount Pleasant for sunset drinks and boat tours

  • Charleston International Airport (CHS) group arrivals and departures

Bachelorette party transportation FAQ

How far ahead should we book bachelorette transportation in Charleston?

Book 4 to 8 weeks ahead for spring and fall weekends, which are Charleston's busiest bachelorette seasons. Summer and winter dates often have more availability, and we can sometimes help with last-minute plans.

Can the chauffeur wait while we're inside a bar or restaurant?

Yes. With an hourly booking your chauffeur stays with the vehicle at each stop and picks the group up when you are ready to move on.

Can we bring drinks on the party bus?

Ask us when you book. Policies follow South Carolina law, and our chauffeur will go over the rules before the ride starts.

Do you pick up from Airbnb and VRBO rentals?

Yes. We pick up from rental houses downtown, in Mount Pleasant, Folly Beach, Isle of Palms, Kiawah Island and anywhere in the Lowcountry.

Do you also do bachelor parties?

Yes. The same vehicles work for bachelor parties, golf days, brewery tours and nights out on King Street.
